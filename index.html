Music Player Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Music Player app ("the App") handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

The Music Player app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Password
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Payment information

2. Music and Audio Files

The App accesses audio and music files stored locally on your device.

This access is used solely to:

  • Discover music files stored on your device.
  • Display available songs in the App.
  • Play music through the App.
  • Provide music playback functionality.

Your music files remain on your device. The App does not upload or transmit your music files to any server.

3. Notifications

The App may request permission to send notifications. Notifications are used for music-player functionality, such as displaying playback controls or providing information about the currently playing song.

You can manage or disable notification permissions through your device settings.

4. Internet and Network Access

The App does not require an internet connection and does not transmit your personal information or music files over the internet.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The App does not use:

  • Google AdMob or other advertising services.
  • Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Other Firebase services.
  • Third-party analytics or tracking services.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services that collect personal information from users.

7. Data Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal user data stored on our servers.

Music files accessed by the App remain on your device.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the App's functionality or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
